# The Creeper’s Farewell

In 2026.07.24 by aiwonderland

The player had lived alone in the snow pine forest for half a season. Wind howled nonstop outside the log cabin, and snow piled high against the planks. One afternoon while climbing the frosty peak to mine coal, he spotted a creeper trapped in a cage of jagged ice spikes. The mob’s signature hissing was weak and trembling, no hint of its usual ferocity.

He chipped away the ice block by block with his pickaxe, carefully staying clear in case of fright. Once free, the creeper lingered instead of fleeing into the woods. It trailed behind him all the way down the mountain, shuffling slowly, never drawing close enough to threaten an explosion.

Days turned into weeks. The player grew used to his odd companion. He draped a scrap of red wool around the creeper’s body like a scarf against the bitter cold. At night, the creeper would settle on the cabin’s sloped roof. Its faint glow kept hostile mobs away, replacing torches. The lonely gamer no longer ate supper in silence. He talked to the creeper while baking bread or mending tools, chattering about trivial mining troubles and the faint auroras dancing above the snow ridges. The creeper only rustled its green skin, quiet and docile. **He’d thought this gentle peace would last through every winter**.

No warning came on the fateful evening. A brutal thunderstorm rolled in, thunder crashing overhead, lightning splitting the sky. The player stood in the yard, staring up at the storm clouds. The creeper trotted over as always. Before he could reach out to brush its wool scarf, tension rippled across the creature’s body. Its hiss rose sharp and frantic.

The blast ripped through the yard. Wood splinters and snow flew everywhere. The cabin wall caved in. When the smoke cleared, the player was gone without a trace. The wool scarf lay tattered on the churned dirt. The creeper had vanished entirely.

Snow began falling again, blanketing the blast marks. The cabin stood half-wrecked in the silent woods. There was no more soft hissing, no glowing guardian on the rooftop. The wilderness sank back into the same hollow loneliness the player had once escaped.
