# Firework Achievements

Written on 2026.07.24 by aiwonderland

The villagers of this Minecraft plains village spent years striving for stable lives. Most residents devoted their time to farming wheat, digging coal and iron underground, and piling cobblestones to thicken the surrounding walls. These plain chores kept them safe from wandering zombies and guaranteed enough food to get through long rainy nights. The village head, however, rarely participated in fieldwork or defense construction. All resources collected collectively by the village were put under his unified control.

He gradually diverted timber, gunpowder, iron and even a small portion of grain savings to build dozens of tall firework launchers scattered along the main roads and the village square. The villagers murmured among themselves, yet no one dared to openly oppose the administrator’s decisions. They could only watch grain bins shrink little by little, while the outer defensive walls aged, chipped and dotted with unfilled holes.

Inspection visits arrived regularly. On these designated days, the administrator ordered all launchers to operate nonstop from sunrise to sunset. Streaks of red, purple and gold fireworks bloomed continuously in the sky. Bright lights echoed across the plains, making the tiny village look prosperous and vibrant from any distant viewpoint. Inspectors wandered the square, praised the village’s thriving atmosphere, wrote down glowing evaluations, and left without venturing into remote alleys or checking storage barns.

Many villagers grew anxious. A group of elder villagers took the courage to negotiate. They begged the administrator to reserve materials for wall repairs and grain reserves, arguing long-term safety mattered more than momentary splendor. The administrator smiled and declined politely. He claimed brilliant fireworks represented the village’s vitality, and high recognition from inspectors would bring more hidden benefits to everyone eventually. The villagers had no way to argue and could only compromise.

Nighttime zombie invasions became more frequent. Walls could no longer block monsters effectively. Villagers locked their wooden doors tightly every evening, suffering constant fear. Everyone endured hardship quietly, convincing themselves that official praise might bring tangible rewards someday.

The long-awaited reward notice finally arrived. The inspectors granted a large batch of building supplies as an award for the village’s outstanding “prosperous appearance”. All villagers rejoiced, assuming these new materials would be used to mend walls and fill granaries.
The administrator transported all awarded materials overnight. The next morning, villagers rushed outside expecting construction work. They found eight brand-new, larger firework launchers standing in the open field.

The administrator stood beside the new machines and announced calmly: the extra supplies were used to upgrade fireworks. Better shows would win more awards in future inspections.

Darkness fell again. The damaged walls creaked in the wind. The villagers stared at the gleaming new launchers, listening to zombies groaning not far beyond the broken walls. No one spoke a word.
